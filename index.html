We develop computational and AI-driven approaches to understand and design molecular systems across energy, catalysis, and biomolecular science.

Research Directions

Our group combines quantum chemistry, molecular dynamics, multiscale modeling, and machine learning to study molecular mechanisms and design principles in complex chemical systems.

AI

AI for Molecular Science

Integrating molecular simulation, data-driven modeling, and machine learning for molecular discovery.

MD

Multiscale Simulation

Connecting electronic structure, atomistic dynamics, and mesoscale behavior in complex systems.

EN

Energy & Catalysis

Understanding reaction mechanisms, interfaces, and molecular design principles for energy applications.

BIO

Biomolecular Systems

Modeling receptors, proteins, and bio-inspired molecular systems to understand structure-function relationships.
