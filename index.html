Research Directions

Exploring matter across scales

We integrate fundamental theory and advanced simulations to understand complex molecular and materials systems.

멀티스케일 계산과학 연구실은 양자역학 계산과 분자동역학을 포함한 멀티스케일 시뮬레이션 및 AI 기반 방법론을 활용해 에너지, 촉매, 나노소재 및 생체분자 시스템의 작동 원리를 이해하고 새로운 소재 설계의 가능성을 탐구합니다.

Battery & Energy Materials

Understanding interfacial processes, ion transport, and reaction mechanisms for advanced energy materials.

Nanomaterials & Catalysis

Exploring structure–property relationships and catalytic mechanisms for functional nanomaterials.

Biomolecular Systems

Investigating biomolecular dynamics, interactions, and bio-inspired materials for biological function and materials design.

Multiscale Modeling

Integrating physics-based simulations and AI-assisted methods across electronic, atomistic, and larger scales.
