Opportunities

Join Our Group

We welcome motivated students and researchers interested in computational science, molecular simulations, and materials modeling.

Our research spans energy materials, nanomaterials and catalysis, biomolecular systems, and the development of multiscale computational methods.

Students and researchers with backgrounds in chemistry, physics, materials science, chemical engineering, or related fields are encouraged to contact us. Please introduce yourself and share your research interests and relevant experience.
